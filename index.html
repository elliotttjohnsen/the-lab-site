Until then

Heists, ranked honestly

GTA Online heists by payout per hour, setup cost and crew size — with the figures labelled as community-reported, estimated or verified, so you know which ones to trust.

The rule the whole app runs on

Every number carries a label.

GTA VI is not out yet, so almost everything anyone says about it is a guess of some kind. The Lab does not hide that. A figure is either confirmed by Rockstar, reported by the community and cross-checked, estimated from a formula we can show you, projected by the assistant, or unverified — and it says which. The assistant is told never to state an unlabelled number as fact, and it will tell you when it does not know.
